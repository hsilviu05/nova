# NOVA Hardware

**Phase 6.**

- `cad/` — printable body, head, and mounts for a Bambu Lab printer
- `electronics/` — wiring diagrams and pin assignments
- `assembly/` — build instructions

Target: ~12–15 cm tall, original design, not humanoid. Budget roughly €50–70.
The bill of materials and the reasoning behind each part is in the
[README](../README.md#hardware).

V1 does not walk. Legged locomotion consumes most of the mechanical budget and
delivers the least; expressive head movement, vision, and voice come first.
